ABOUT US

At YOGA STUDIO, we believe that yoga is more than just a practice—it's a journey towards inner peace, strength, and self-discovery. Our mission is to create a welcoming space where individuals of all ages and skill levels can connect with their bodies, calm their minds, and nourish their souls. Founded by passionate yogis, our studio is dedicated to offering a wide range of classes, from gentle restorative yoga to invigorating vinyasa flows. Our experienced instructors are committed to guiding you through each step of your yoga journey, whether you're a seasoned practitioner or stepping onto the mat for the first time. Join us to explore the transformative power of yoga and embark on a path to holistic well-being. At THE YOGA BUZZ, you'll find a supportive community that inspires growth, balance, and harmony—both on and off the mat. Namaste.



Our Classes

BEGGINER YOGA

Perfect for those new to yoga, focusing on basic poses and breathing techniques.

VINYASA YOGA

A dynamic and flowing style of yoga, connecting breath with movement.

RESTORATIVE YOGA

Relax and unwind with gentle stretches and deep relaxation.

HATHA YOGA

A traditional form of yoga focusing on basic postures.

KUNDALINI YOGA

A spiritual and meditative form aimed at awakening the energy at the base of the spine

YIN YOGA

A slow-paced style with deep stretches held for longer periods (3-5 minutes).

POWER YOGA

A more intense, fitness-oriented version of Vinyasa Yoga.
